GOAL: Design a behavioral intervention using the EAST Framework.

INPUT:

EAST FRAMEWORK:
- Easy: Remove friction.
- Attractive: Attract attention.
- Social: Show that others perform the behavior.
- Timely: Intervene at the right time.

INSTRUCTIONS:

5. Add a Revision Question: To check if the user agrees.
